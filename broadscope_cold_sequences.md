## 🛑 STRATEGY UPDATE: The Dual-Channel Approach

The messaging strategy has been split to respect the medium:

1. **Cold Email (The Professional Disarm):** Emails sent on behalf of BroadScope must sound like professional B2B systems architects speaking to executives. The "sales disarm" is achieved through extreme technical relevance, not casual language. We state why we are reaching out, prove we understand their specific tech stack, and offer a concrete technical outcome.
2. **LinkedIn (The Founder-to-Founder Mentorship Hook):** Reserved specifically for Albert Jere (BroadScope CEO). This channel leverages the "Deep Operator" model. Albert reaches out casually, founder-to-founder, asking for feedback on architecture to spark high-intent conversations.
